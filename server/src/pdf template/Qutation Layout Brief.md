# Project Brief: Neptune Premium Planters Digital Quotation

## Project Overview
**Objective:** Create a high-fidelity, production-ready digital quotation system for Neptune Premium Planters that bridges the gap between a technical invoice and a luxury design proposal.
**Brand Identity:** Architectural, sophisticated, and premium. The design must reflect "Crafted Spaces. Timeless Design."

## Visual Identity & Design System
*   **Design System Name:** Architectural Elegance
*   **Primary Palette:** 
    *   **Rich Black (#171717):** Used for structural blocks, headers, and the "Grand Total" section to establish authority and luxury.
    *   **Luxury Gold (#C9A15D):** Used for iconography, dividers, and key highlights to provide an upscale accent.
    *   **Alabaster White (#FFFFFF):** Primary background for maximum clarity and whitespace.
    *   **Surface Cream (#F9F6F1):** Subtle background for testimonial and quote blocks.
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Headings & Accents:** *Playfair Display* (Serif). Used for high-impact titles (QUOTATION), testimonials, and the final amount to convey elegance.
    *   **Body & Technical Data:** *Inter* (Sans-Serif). Used for addresses, labels, and the product table to ensure clinical legibility and modern utility.

## Layout & Structure (Desktop)
1.  **Header Hero:**
    *   Asymmetric two-column layout.
    *   Left: A dark trapezoidal block with gold diagonal border containing the brand logo and tagline.
    *   Focal Point: A high-resolution planter asset overlapping the dark/light boundary with soft realistic shadows.
    *   Right: Large "QUOTATION" header with vertical quotation details (No, Date, Terms) paired with minimalist gold icons.
2.  **Information Section:**
    *   Clean three-column grid for "Bill To," "Ship To," and a "Quote Block."
    *   Quote Block: Encased in a light cream box with a large decorative quotation mark and italicized serif text.
3.  **Advanced Product Table:**
    *   Professional black header row with gold labels.
    *   Multi-column structure: Product Image, Description, Specifications (Size/Finish), Qty, Unit Price, Total.
    *   *Visual Suite Integration:* Additional columns for "Reference Image" (macro architectural inspiration) and "Stone Texture" (high-res material samples) to assist client visualization.
4.  **Financials & Footer:**
    *   Standard Subtotal/GST breakdown leading to a high-contrast black "Grand Total" card.
    *   Signature area with space for stamps and prepared-by details.
    *   Global footer bar in black with gold icons for contact and location details.

## Technical Specifications
*   **Grid:** 1200px centered container with generous whitespace (architectural margins).
*   **Assets:** All product images must be studio-lit on transparent backgrounds. Material samples should be 1:1 square crops.
*   **Interactivity:** Responsive desktop-first design, optimized for high-resolution displays and professional printing (PDF export).

## Design Constraints
*   **No Generic UI:** Avoid Bootstrap-style buttons, rounded SaaS cards, or vibrant gradients.
*   **Negative Constraints:** No sidebars, no excessive shadows, no decorative backgrounds that detract from content.
*   **Fidelity:** 1:1 visual match with established brand assets and spacing established in the Architectural Elegance design system.
